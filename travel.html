How to Get There

Travel Options

Multiple ways to reach our island paradise

Fastest Route

✈️ Fly Direct

Athens → Kythera (KIT Airport)
Olympic Air operates direct flights from Athens International Airport. The flight takes just 40 minutes and offers spectacular aerial views of the Greek coastline.

Tip: Book early! Summer flights fill up fast. Flights run daily in September.

~€80-150 round trip

🦝
Scenic Route

⛴️ Ferry from Neapoli

Neapoli (Peloponnese) → Kythera
The most popular ferry route! Departs from Neapoli in the southern Peloponnese. The crossing takes about 1 hour and you can bring your car aboard.

Tip: Lane Sea Lines runs this route. Check schedules at ferries.gr for September timetables.

~€12 per person / €40 with car

Adventure Route

🚢 Ferry from Crete

Kissamos (Crete) → Kythera
Combine your trip with a visit to Crete! Seasonal ferries connect Kissamos to Kythera in about 2.5 hours, passing through the beautiful Antikythera island.

Tip: This route runs weekly in September. Perfect for a Crete + Kythera combo trip!

~€20 per person

Step One

Getting to Athens

Your gateway to Greece — the adventure starts here

Athens International Airport (ATH)

Athens is served by direct flights from most major cities worldwide. Airlines like Aegean Air, British Airways, Lufthansa, Delta, Emirates, and many budget carriers fly direct.

From major hubs:

  • 🇬🇧 London → Athens: ~3.5 hours
  • 🇩🇪 Berlin → Athens: ~3 hours
  • 🇫🇷 Paris → Athens: ~3.5 hours
  • 🇺🇸 New York → Athens: ~10 hours
  • 🇦🇺 Sydney → Athens: ~20 hours (1 stop)

We recommend arriving in Athens 1-2 days before heading to Kythera to explore this incredible city and adjust to the time zone!

Day 1 · Thursday, Sept 3

Arrival & Settle In

Fly from Athens to Kythera in the morning. Check into your hotel, have a leisurely lunch at a seaside taverna in Kapsali, and spend the afternoon exploring Chora's cobblestone streets. Evening: welcome drinks with fellow guests at a local bar. 🍹

Day 2 · Friday, Sept 4

Explore & Pre-Wedding Fun

Morning beach time at one of Kythera's stunning beaches. Afternoon: visit the Venetian castle and the cave of Agia Sofia. Evening: pre-wedding dinner and celebration with the wedding party. Maybe a sneaky midnight swim? 🌙

Day 3 · Saturday, Sept 5

💍 THE WEDDING DAY!

Relax and pamper yourselves in the morning. Ceremony at 4:00 PM at the clifftop chapel as the golden hour begins. Reception, dinner, dancing, and celebration under the stars until dawn. This is going to be EPIC! 🎉

Day 1 · Tuesday, Sept 1

Athens Arrival

Land in Athens, check into a boutique hotel in Plaka. Walk through the ancient Agora, gaze up at the Acropolis, and enjoy dinner at a rooftop restaurant with views of the Parthenon lit up at night. ✨

Day 2 · Wednesday, Sept 2

Athens to Kythera

Morning flight to Kythera (40 min). Check into your accommodation. Afternoon: first swim at Kapsali beach, followed by a traditional Greek lunch. Sunset stroll through Chora. Evening at leisure — maybe catch the sunset from the castle walls.

Day 3 · Thursday, Sept 3

Beach Hopping Day

Rent a car and explore the island's incredible beaches: Kaladi (dramatic cliffs), Komponada (secluded paradise), and Fyri Ammos (golden sand). Pack a picnic or stop at a beach cantina. Evening: group dinner with wedding guests arriving.

Day 4 · Friday, Sept 4

Culture & Pre-Wedding Party

Visit Mylopotamos village and the stunning waterfall. Explore the cave of Agia Sofia with its Byzantine frescoes. Afternoon: free time to relax and get ready. Evening: pre-wedding celebration and rehearsal dinner overlooking the sea. 🌊

Day 5 · Saturday, Sept 5

💍 THE WEDDING DAY!

The big day! Morning spa treatments and relaxation. Ceremony at 4:00 PM with the most incredible sunset backdrop. Reception dinner, first dance, Greek dancing, and pure joy until the early hours. Opa! 💃🕺

Day 1-2 · Aug 30-31

Athens Experience

Two days in Athens! Visit the Acropolis, the National Archaeological Museum, wander through Monastiraki flea market, eat souvlaki in Psyrri, and sip cocktails on rooftop bars. Athens is a city that deserves your time.

Day 3 · Monday, Sept 1

Road Trip to Neapoli

Rent a car and drive south through the Peloponnese (~3.5 hours of stunning scenery). Stop at ancient Sparta or the Byzantine castle town of Monemvasia along the way. Overnight in charming Neapoli on the coast.

Day 4 · Tuesday, Sept 2

Ferry to Kythera

Morning ferry from Neapoli to Kythera (1 hour). The approach by sea is magical — the island rising from the blue. Check in, beach time, first taverna dinner with island vibes. Pure Mediterranean bliss. 🌊

Day 5 · Wednesday, Sept 3

Kythera Deep Dive

Full island exploration: the abandoned village of Paleochora, the bridge of Katouni (largest stone bridge in Greece), honey tasting at a local apiary, and swimming in the natural pool at Mylopotamos waterfall. Evening: seafood feast in Avlemonas fishing village. 🦞

Day 6 · Thursday, Sept 4

Beach Day & Pre-Wedding

Morning at the spectacular Kaladi beach. Afternoon: free time, spa, getting pampered. Evening: pre-wedding party with all guests — cocktails, music, laughter, and the excitement of tomorrow! 🎶

Day 7 · Friday, Sept 5

💍 THE WEDDING DAY!

The moment we've all been waiting for! After a week of Greek adventures, new friendships, and incredible memories, we celebrate love in the most beautiful setting imaginable. Ceremony, feast, dancing — forever! 🎉💕

Good to Know

Travel Tips

Practical advice to make your trip smooth and stress-free

🚗

Rent a Car

We strongly recommend renting a car on Kythera. The island has limited public transport and a car gives you the freedom to explore beaches and villages at your own pace. Book ahead for September!

💰

Currency & Payment

Greece uses the Euro (€). Most restaurants and hotels accept cards, but bring some cash for small tavernas, beach bars, and village shops. ATMs are available in Chora and a few larger villages.

📱

Connectivity

EU citizens: your phone plan works in Greece! Others: get a local SIM at Athens airport (Cosmote or Vodafone). WiFi is available at most hotels and restaurants on the island.

🧴

What to Pack

Sunscreen (essential!), comfortable walking shoes, swimwear, a light jacket for evenings, your wedding outfit 👗, and a sense of adventure. September evenings can be breezy!

🏠

Where to Stay

We've arranged special rates at a few lovely hotels near the wedding venue. Airbnb also has beautiful traditional houses. We'll send accommodation details in the invitation!

🦝
🗣️

Language

Greek is the official language, but English is widely spoken in tourist areas. Learn a few words: Yassou (Hello), Efcharistó (Thank you), Opa! (Cheers!), and Pame! (Let's go!).
