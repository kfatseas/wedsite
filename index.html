A Chapter Written by Fate

It started with a chance encounter — a crowded café, a shared table, and a conversation that neither of us wanted to end. What began as a simple hello turned into hours of laughter, and hours turned into a lifetime of love.

From late-night adventures to lazy Sunday mornings, from our first trip to Greece to the moment we knew Kythera would be our place — every step has been an adventure we wouldn't trade for the world.

And now, surrounded by the Aegean Sea and the people we love most, we're ready to begin our greatest adventure yet.
