A Chapter Written by Fate

It all started with a cup of coffee. A grande cappuccino on cold days, and a huge caramel frappuccino when the sun came out.

What began as quick exchanges across the counter slowly turned into lingering lunch breaks, shared tables with friends, and the kind of easy conversation you don't want to end. Somehow, talking about absolutely nothing — cats, trees, the weather, whatever — turned out to be the beginning of everything.

Eventually, we couldn't resist admitting what we already knew. A 15-minute coffee break turned into a lifetime of being inseparable.

Forever starts here.

The Ceremony

At the church on the castle of Chora — one of the most iconic spots on the island, perched above the beautiful beach of Kapsali with sweeping views across to the islet of Hytra.

5:30 PM · Chora Castle 🏰

🥂

The Reception

At the square of Mitata — one of Kythera's oldest and greenest villages, renowned for its wine production and beloved wine festival. Expect dinner, dancing, and celebration surrounded by lush hills and vineyard views.

7:00 PM – 3:00 AM · Mitata Village 🍷

🏨

Accommodation

Coming from abroad? We've reserved accommodation in Livadi, the vibrant town where Costas grew up. It has cafés, bars, restaurants, a nail salon, a gym, and great connections to the rest of the island. We have reserved accommodation for 3 nights. If you'd like to turn the trip into a longer holiday, we highly recommend booking extra nights early. 🌿

Livadi · 3 Nights Reserved 🏡

🧥

What to Wear

No dress code — come as you are! Just note that Kythera evenings can turn breezy, so we recommend bringing a light vest or thin sweater. Expect 27–30°C during the day and a lovely 20–23°C at night.
