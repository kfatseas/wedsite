The Hidden Gem of Greece

Kythera (Κύθηρα) is one of Greece's best-kept secrets — a stunning island floating between the Peloponnese and Crete, untouched by mass tourism and dripping with mythological significance.

According to ancient Greek mythology, Kythera is the birthplace of Aphrodite, the goddess of love. What better place to celebrate a wedding?

With its whitewashed villages cascading down hillsides, hidden beaches with turquoise waters, Venetian castles, and legendary sunsets, Kythera is a destination that will steal your heart.

Why Kythera?

Here's why we chose this magical island for our wedding

💕

Island of Love

As Aphrodite's birthplace, Kythera is literally the island of love. The ancient Greeks believed love itself originated here — the perfect backdrop for our forever.

🏖️

Unspoiled Beauty

Unlike more touristy Greek islands, Kythera remains wonderfully authentic. No cruise ships, no party resorts — just pure, breathtaking natural beauty.

🦝
🏛️

Rich History

Venetian castles, Byzantine churches, and centuries of fascinating history around every corner. The island has been influenced by Greeks, Venetians, British, and more.

🌅

September Magic

September in Kythera offers warm days (25°C), calm seas, golden light, and that magical end-of-summer feeling. Absolutely perfect wedding weather.

🍽️

Amazing Food

From fresh-caught seafood to local honey and wild herbs, Kythera's cuisine is a Mediterranean dream. Wait until you taste the wedding feast!

Intimate & Special

Must See

Places to Explore

Make the most of your time on this magical island

Chora (Kythera Town)

The island's enchanting capital clings to a hilltop crowned by a magnificent Venetian castle. Wander through narrow cobblestone streets, discover hidden cafés, and take in panoramic views that stretch to the horizon.

Don't miss the castle at sunset — it's one of the most magical experiences in all of Greece.

Kapsali Beach

A stunning twin bay right below the castle of Chora. Picture-perfect turquoise waters, a charming harbor, and several excellent tavernas where you can enjoy fresh fish with your feet nearly in the sand.

Mylopotamos

A fairytale village with a Venetian-era watermill, lush vegetation, and the incredible waterfall of Fonissa — a hidden natural pool perfect for a cool dip.
